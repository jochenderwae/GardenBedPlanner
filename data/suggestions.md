- 2026-07-19: **`title_case_plant_name` (`data/etl/normalize.py`) has two remaining cosmetic edge cases**, found running task 5 for real (fixed the two clearer bugs directly - leading punctuation like `"(Tulsi)"` never got capitalized, and a word after an internal `/` never got capitalized either, e.g. `"Melon (Honeydew/Specialty)"` -> `"...(Honeydew/specialty)"` - both real regressions against already-correctly-cased source data, not just missed improvements, since the normalizer ran and silently mangled a value that was fine before.
