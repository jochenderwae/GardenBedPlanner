# User journeys

## User Dave
Dave is a father of three and doesn't have a lot of time to spend on his garden. Gardening is his main hobby and he spends what little time he has working his average sized garden.
Dave has a back condition and can't work the land at ground level, he will mainly use raised beds and planters.
Dave is concerned about the environment his children will inherit and will try, where possible, to use water sparingly and try to use eco friendly gardening techniques: so seeds and plants from organic cultivation, using compost and only sparingly eco friendly fertiliser, absolutely no pesticides or herbicides.
Dave is tech savvy and tries to find time saving solutions where possible (like automatic drip irrigation instead of manually spraying the whole garden). He also needs an easy way to keep track of his todo's in the garden: 5 minutes of free time should be used as efficiently possible (and focused on the most urgent tasks)
